# Demo script: practice dashboard (M2)

For a 10-minute session with a practising CA. Goal: learn what to build next, not to sell.
Open `/practice` on the deployed site (no login). Press **Reset demo** before you start.

## Walkthrough (5 minutes)

1. **Dashboard.** "This is a firm with ten clients for FY 2024-25. At a glance: seven are waiting on clients, one needs your review, one is ready to file, one is filed." Point at the dots: one per required document, hollow = missing, red = a problem.
2. **Remind.** On *Fatima Khan* press **Remind**. The message lists only what's missing, with the reason. **Open in WhatsApp** sends it from your own number.
3. **Checklist.** Open *Neha Kapoor*, then **Checklist**: two employers, so two Form 16s; one is in. Untick and tick profile boxes to show the list change.
4. **Client sends it.** On *Vikram Mehta*'s checklist press **Client sends it** for the capital-gains statement. It is read, the gains appear in **Review facts** and **Tax computation**, and the dashboard moves him to *Needs review*.
5. **Issues.** Open *Rahul Sharma*: documents are in, but red dots mean high-priority questions (cash deposit, AIS interest mismatch). Open **Flags** to show the client question the system drafted.
6. **New client.** **New client** → tick what their year looks like → the checklist is built for you.

## Questions to ask (write the answers down verbatim)

1. Does the document list match what you actually ask clients for? What's missing or wrong?
2. Which client profile boxes would you add (e.g. NRI, crypto, foreign assets, agricultural income, senior citizen, presumptive 44AD/ADA)?
3. How do you chase documents today, and how many rounds does a typical client take?
4. Would you send reminders from your own WhatsApp, or prefer the firm's business number with automatic follow-ups?
5. Would clients use an upload link, or will they always send documents on WhatsApp?
6. What would make "Ready to file" trustworthy enough that you'd skip re-checking?
7. What would you pay per month for this dashboard plus reminders? Per firm or per client?
8. Which one thing would you want next: client upload link, automatic reminders, new-Act (Tax Year 2026-27) rules, or tax-news alerts per client?

## After the session
Add the answers to `docs/product/feedback/` (one file per CA, no client names), then re-rank M3–M7 in `SAAS-RESEARCH-AND-PLAN.md`.
